# PrintEase Privacy Policy

PrintEase does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

Files you choose to print or scan (photos, documents, web pages, email attachments) are processed only on your device and are sent only to the printer you select through the Android print system. Scanned documents are saved locally on your device and can be deleted in the app at any time.

PrintEase does not use analytics, advertising, or user accounts.

The document scanner is provided by Google ML Kit and runs on your device.

# PrintEase Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

PrintEase does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

## How your files are handled
Files you choose to print or scan (photos, documents, web pages, email attachments) are processed only on your device and are sent only to the printer you select through the Android print system. We do not receive a copy.

Scanned documents are saved locally on your device and can be deleted in the app at any time. Uninstalling the app removes them.

## Document scanner
The document scanner is provided by Google ML Kit and runs on your device. Scanned images are not uploaded by PrintEase.

## What the app does not use
PrintEase does not use analytics, advertising, tracking, or user accounts.

## Permissions
- Camera: only to scan documents.
- Photos and files (or the system file picker): only to print what you select.
- Network: to reach your printer and load web pages you choose to print.

You can revoke permissions at any time in your device settings.

## Third-party print services
When you print, the print service or plugin of your printer manufacturer installed on your device may process the document under its own privacy policy. We do not control these services.

## Children
PrintEase is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone.

## Changes
If this policy changes, we will update it and the date above.
